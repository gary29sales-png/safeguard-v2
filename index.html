SafeGuard is available on most private and light commercial vehicles, subject to the following conditions.

✓ Eligible vehicles
  • Private light passenger vehicles
  • 4x4s and minibuses (≤3 500 kg GVM)
  • Light delivery vehicles (≤4 500 kg GVM)
  • Panel vans (≤4 500 kg GVM)
  • Comprehensively insured with a registered South African insurer
  • Approved window film fitted at an authorised centre
✗ Not eligible
  • Taxis or e-hailing vehicles (Uber, Bolt, etc.)
  • Cash-in-transit vehicles
  • Hire vehicles
  • Rebuilt or modified vehicles
  • Motorsport or competition vehicles
  • Vehicles without underlying comprehensive insurance
  • Vehicles not fitted with authorised safety film

All claims are handled by Traficc. Here's exactly what to do after an incident.

1
Ensure your safety first
Get yourself and any passengers to a safe location. Call emergency services if anyone is injured.
2
File a SAPS report
Report the incident at your nearest SAPS station and ensure every stolen or damaged item is listed on the case docket. Items not on the SAPS report cannot be included in your claim.
Within 48 hours of the incident
3
Contact the Traficc claims team
Call 0861 872 3422 or email claims@traficc.co.za (Mon–Fri, 08:30–17:00). Have your policy schedule and SAPS case number ready.
Submit within 31 days of the incident
4
Complete the claim form
Traficc will provide you with a claim form and guide you through exactly what supporting documentation is needed for each benefit you're claiming.
5
Loyalty bonus claim
Applicable if your vehicle had an active Vehicle Tracking and Recovery Device, with a registered South African tracking company, at the time of being stolen or hijacked. If your vehicle is not recovered, or is recovered but written off, Guardrisk pays an amount towards a replacement vehicle of your choice from the dealer group or dealership noted on your policy.
Must be claimed within 90 days
